# 🎤 Voice AI Usage Guide - Quick Start

## 🚀 **HOW TO USE THE CONVERSATIONAL VOICE AI**

---

## 🎯 **Two Ways to Use**

### **Method 1: Click to Talk** (Default)
1. Click the 🎤 button in header
2. Speak your command
3. AI responds and asks questions
4. Keep clicking and speaking for multi-turn conversation

### **Method 2: Wake Word** (Hands-free)
1. Say "أبشر" (Absher)
2. AI responds: "نعم، كيف يمكنني مساعدتك؟"
3. Say your command
4. Continue conversation

---

## 💬 **Example Conversations**

### **Scenario 1: Check Violations** 🚗

```
YOU: "أبشر المخالفات"
AI: "حسناً، سأساعدك في الاستعلام عن المخالفات.
     ما نوع الرقم الذي تريد البحث به؟ 
     رقم الهوية أم رقم اللوحة؟"

YOU: "رقم الهوية"
AI: "ممتاز. الآن قل رقم الهوية من فضلك، رقم برقم بوضوح"

YOU: "واحد اثنين ثلاثة اربعة خمسة ستة سبعة ثمانية تسعة صفر"
AI: "رقم الهوية 1234567890. هل هذا صحيح؟ قل نعم أو لا"

YOU: "نعم"
AI: "جاري البحث عن المخالفات..."
→ Opens violations page with your ID filled in!
```

### **Scenario 2: Renew ID Card** 🆔

```
YOU: "تجديد الهوية"

YOU: "1234567890"
AI: "رقم الهوية 1234567890. هل معك صورة الهوية؟ قل نعم أو لا"

YOU: "نعم"

     استخدم الكاميرا لتصوير الهوية"

```

### **Scenario 3: Passport Services** 🛂

```
YOU: "جواز سفر"
AI: "خدمات جواز السفر. هل تريد: إصدار جديد، تجديد، أو استعلام؟"

YOU: "إصدار جديد"
AI: "سأفتح صفحة جواز السفر - إصدار جديد"
→ Opens passport page!
```

### **Scenario 4: Quick Navigation** 🏠

```
YOU: "الخدمات"

→ Navigates to services page!

YOU: "الرئيسية"
```

## 🗣️ **What You Can Say**

### **Services:**
- "المخالفات" or "violations" → Check traffic violations
- "تجديد الهوية" or "renew ID" → Renew national ID
- "جواز السفر" or "passport" → Passport services
- "رخصة القيادة" or "driving license" → Driving license
- "تسجيل المركبة" or "vehicle" → Vehicle registration
- "التوظيف" or "employment" → Employment services
- "الصحة" or "health" → Health services
- "التعليم" or "education" → Education services

### **Navigation:**
- "الخدمات" or "services" → Go to services page
- "الرئيسية" or "home" → Go to home page

### **Help:**
- "مساعدة" or "help" → Show available services
- Click 💜 button → Show voice commands list

### **Responses:**
- "نعم" or "yes" → Confirm
- "لا" or "no" → Decline/retry
- Say numbers: "واحد اثنين ثلاثة..." → Enter ID numbers

## 🎯 **Step-by-Step Guide**

### **First Time Use:**

1. **Allow Microphone**
   - Browser will ask for microphone permission
   - Click "Allow" ✅

2. **Click 🎤 Button**
   - Located in top-right header
   - Button turns blue when listening

3. **Speak Clearly**
   - Say "أبشر" + your command
   - Or just say the service name

4. **Follow AI Prompts**
   - AI will ask questions
   - Answer naturally in Arabic or English

5. **Confirm Actions**
   - AI will confirm before executing
   - Say "نعم" to proceed

6. **Done!**
   - AI navigates you automatically
   - Or fills forms for you

---

## 💡 **Pro Tips**

### ✅ **DO:**
- Speak clearly and at normal pace
- Wait for AI to finish speaking before responding
- Use Arabic or English (both work!)
- Say numbers digit by digit: "واحد، اثنين، ثلاثة..."
- Confirm with "نعم" when asked

### ❌ **DON'T:**
- Don't speak while AI is talking (causes echo)
- Don't mumble or speak too fast
- Don't interrupt AI mid-sentence
- Don't say numbers all at once: ❌ "واحد اثنين ثلاثة اربعة خمسة..."
  Instead, pause between digits: ✅ "واحد... اثنين... ثلاثة..."

## 🔧 **Troubleshooting**

### **Problem: AI doesn't hear me**
**Solution:**
1. Check microphone permission (allow in browser)
2. Speak louder and clearer
3. Reduce background noise
4. Click mic button and try again

### **Problem: AI misunderstands me**
**Solution:**
1. Speak more clearly
2. Use simpler words
3. Say command again when AI asks
4. Use English if Arabic not working

### **Problem: Conversation stuck**
**Solution:**
1. Say "مساعدة" or "help"
2. Or refresh page (F5)
3. Click mic and start over

### **Problem: No response**
**Solution:**
1. Check internet connection
2. Refresh page
3. Check browser console for errors

## 🎨 **Visual Indicators**

### **Button Colors:**
- **Gray 🎤** - Idle (ready to use)
- **Blue 🎤** - Listening (speak now!)
- **Green 🎤** - Active conversation (multi-turn)
- **Orange 🎤** - Processing (AI thinking...)
- **Red 🎤** - Error (try again)

### **Waveform:**
- Animated blue line below header when listening
- Shows AI is actively listening

### **Status Badge:**
- "جاهز" - Ready
- "أستمع..." - Listening
- "نشط" - Active conversation
- "معالجة..." - Processing
- "خطأ" - Error
